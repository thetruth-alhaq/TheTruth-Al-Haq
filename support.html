Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable prayer time alerts?

Go to Prayer Times screen, tap the bell icon next to each prayer you want alerts for. Make sure notification permissions are granted.

Why are my prayer times not accurate?

Tap the location icon in the Prayer Times screen to detect your current location. Prayer times are calculated based on your GPS coordinates.

How do I download audio for offline use?

Go to Settings > Audio Download Settings to pre-download Quran recitations for offline listening.

The app is in the wrong language

Tap the language icon on the home screen to change the app language. We support 16 languages including Arabic, English, French, Urdu, and more.
